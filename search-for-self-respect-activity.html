Fourteen-Week Goal Builders

Chapter 15 is a program: fourteen Goal Builders, one a week, six days on and one off — 84 working days in all. Each day: do the week's exercise, write its word on your mirror, say its sentence, then act (pp. 231–232).

1

This week's Goal Builder

Give each goal builder one full week: work on it for six days, then take a day off (p. 231). Pick the one you're on.

2

Do the exercise

Most Goal Builders start in a quiet place with your eyes closed and your imagination at work; some use pencil and paper (pp. 232–248). Maltz made several of them purposely exaggerated and unreal so that when you come back to earth you can cope (p. 248).

3

Write it on the mirror

Take out a lipstick or an erasable pencil and in large capital letters—don't be afraid—write on the mirror (p. 233). Look at the word and at yourself, and say the week's sentence.

Active, not passive

    Set goals every day (pp. 60, 81). Weigh it the way Goal Builder 6 asks: Is it your goal? Does it mean something to you?… Do you have a fighting chance, at least? (p. 240).
