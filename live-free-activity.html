21-Day Data Revision

The book's main tool is "data revision": a written goal, a short affirmation, and a daily few minutes of words, pictures and feelings (pp. 93–97). This page walks you through it each day for 21 days. For the book's closing exercises, see the Live Free Workbook.

    Words, pictures, feelings

    Relax completely, eyes closed. Say the affirmation, picture yourself doing it, and feel what it's like to have done it. Begin by knowing you have arrived (p. 95). Ten minutes a day (p. 117).

    Glance at a negative

    Treat a negative like a light on the instrument panel: an occasional glance, then correct (pp. 126–127). Also notice one phrase like that makes me sick and replace it (p. 98).

    The day in reverse

    Just before sleep, hold your attention on the day's activities in reverse order, from getting into bed back to getting up. It builds the muscles of your 'attention' (p. 121).
