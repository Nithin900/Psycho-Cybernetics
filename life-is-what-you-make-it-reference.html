Life Is What You Make It, at a glance

Look things up: the formulas for worry and for goals, the five things you want from others, the twenty-four attitudes, every chapter's questions, the quotations Willingham leans on and the stories. Book content only, with page numbers.

Ten chapters (pp. 15–154), each ending with “Questions to Ask Yourself.” Willingham, who ran personal-development courses in Amarillo, Texas, writes from a Christian viewpoint and quotes the Bible (mostly The Living Bible) alongside Emerson and Maltz.

Questions to ask yourself

Each chapter ends with these; Willingham asks group leaders to use them.

ChapterQuestionsPage
1How have past fears kept you from launching out? What caused you to be where you are? How can you change your future? Why do thoughts change your life? Which of the 24 attitudes do you need most?29
2How do you react to defeats? Do you feel you have more problems than anyone? Your biggest defeats? What did they teach you? How has fear of failure robbed you?42
3List blessings for ten minutes, troubles for ten — the score? How to be more thankful? Which weaknesses became strengths? What causes your negative and positive thoughts?55
4How have past attitudes shaped your circumstances? Do you go the extra mile? What does "Every act rewards itself" mean to you? What are you doing for a positive attitude? What incident proves the law?68
5What treatment do you want? How often do you take the initiative? Do you react, or cause reactions? Are some unfriendly from fear of rejection? Why do people reciprocate?81
6What is greatness to you? Giver or taker? Can you give without reward? Is it more happiness to give? Why is selfishness a roadblock?94
7How hard is it to ask? Why the fear? How has it shaped your life? What have you done about it? What will you do?108
8Your opinion of yourself? What caused it? How has it shaped your life? Who would you like to be? Your plan?124
9Your greatest fears? Their cause? What can you do? How to grow faith? The more you try, the more problems — what does that mean to you?138
10What does goal-setting mean to you? How often do you set goals? Are they specific? Have you followed the setting formula? The making-them-work formula?154
