The fifteen exercises

Maltz promised "fifteen concrete exercises" (p. 8); he asks you to make each one a daily goal (p. 47).

No.ExerciseWhat you doPages
1Mirror reacquaintanceLinger at the mirror; see the person behind the face and his successes; ask "How is my self-image today?"15–17
2Reason away perfectionismComfortable, reason with yourself: ever met a perfect anyone? Say "I may be a mistake-maker, but I am also a mistake-breaker"34–35
3In a crowdSee a past success in technicolor, then another happy moment, then plan your next goal as you walk46–47
4Your emotional harelipClose the gap with threads of love; live with what can't be fixed; list good qualities; flash a success58–59
5V for VictoryAt the mirror, think of your good moments and make the V sign in your mind70–72
6Cross-examine negative beliefsPut each self-accusation on trial, shrink it, then hold a vivid success image and affirm you're the captain of your ship88–90
7Four ideas for pleasureBuild up your self-image · unearth hidden wealth · give to others · concentrate on your goals98–100
8Tomorrow's goalIn the evening, pick tomorrow's goal, see yourself moving toward it, relive one success and then another115–116
9The double featureA capsizing boat (dwelling on failures), then a boat reaching port (dwelling on successes); feel both128–129
10Escape and forgiveSlow your worries to a stop, take a mental vacation, forgive yourself, others and the world, then set a goal141–142
11Talk it over (11A: let off steam)Discuss the success mechanism, self-image and negative feelings with a trusted friend; 11A: picture the geysers and release anger harmlessly154–156
12Loneliness checkRun down the six symptoms in your mental mirror and work on each166–167
13Age senseTalk sense about age · fight pessimistic talk · set goals · never retire · claim the right to be happy178–179
14A flower for yourselfSend yourself a flower "From an admirer!" and say "I deserve this flower!" at the mirror197–198
15Lights onTurn a light off and on to feel blackout vs vision; then use the first hour for self-image, weeding and reaffirming208–209
