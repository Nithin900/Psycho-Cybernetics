# Psycho-Cybernetics

Study pages on Maxwell Maltz's Psycho-Cybernetics books. Each page is a single HTML file: open it in any browser.

| Page | What it is |
|---|---|
| [`index.html`](index.html) | **Live Free Workbook**: a plain-English workbook for Chapter 27, "Exercises and Techniques", of *Live and Be Free Thru Psycho-Cybernetics* (Maltz and Schreiber, 1975) |
| [`psycho-cybernetics-notes.html`](psycho-cybernetics-notes.html) | **Psycho-Cybernetics Notes**: the 15 principles from theory to practice, SUCCESS vs FAILURE, a daily routine, the four rational questions, *Creative Living for Today*, an evidence check and a reading list |
| [`chapter-2-success-mechanism.html`](chapter-2-success-mechanism.html) | **Chapter 2 Report**: "Discovering the Success Mechanism Within You" |
| [`self-image-code-podcast.html`](self-image-code-podcast.html) | **The Self-Image Code**: a two-host podcast script on Maltz's tools and the research behind them (cold open, Segment 1 and Segment 2) |

## Live Free Workbook contents

- A 15-minute daily routine
- The 12 practice tools from the chapter (the 21-day pledge, mirror practice, red light/green light, the 10 rules of success, decisions, "Just for today")
- "Say what you feel": one-sentence phrases for common feelings
- "Swap your words": better phrases than "I can't" and "I have to"
- A word list with meanings and example sentences
- A 21-day tracker (ticks are saved in your browser)

All pages are plain-English summaries written in my own words, not the books' exact text.
