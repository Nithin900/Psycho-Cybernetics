# Maltz Complete Ideas: Stacked by Theme + a 12-Week Application System

Across his books Maltz teaches **one system**, repeated in different words: 70 ideas that fall into **7 themes**. You don't need all of them at once. Learn the themes in order, practise each for about three weeks, and the whole system takes 12 weeks (see "The 12-week system"). Run it day to day in the [Practice Engine](practice-engine.html).

**The books (codes used below):** PC *Psycho-Cybernetics* (1960) · CLT *Creative Living for Today* (1967) · CF *The Conquest of Frustration* (1969) · PCSF *Psycho-Cybernetics & Self-Fulfillment* (1970) · PCY *Power Psycho-Cybernetics for Youth* · SSR *The Search for Self-Respect* (1973) · LBF *Live and Be Free Thru Psycho-Cybernetics* (1975).

Page numbers for the self-image come from the [Self-Image Ladder](self-image-ladder.html). Some chapter references for the other themes were written from memory and are not yet checked against the books.

## The 7 themes and how they connect

| # | Theme | The one question it answers | Role in the system |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | Self-Image | Who do I believe I am? | The **thermostat**: sets your limit |
| 2 | Success Mechanism | How does my mind reach goals? | The **engine**: steers toward the target you give it |
| 3 | Imagination | How do I change the picture? | The **tool**: rewrites the self-image |
| 4 | Beliefs & Dehypnosis | What false ideas are holding me? | The **cleanup**: removes wrong programming |
| 5 | Relaxation | How do I let the engine work? | The **condition**: change happens relaxed, not forced |
| 6 | Emotions | How do I handle bad feelings and old hurts? | The **dashboard**: warning lights + repairs |
| 7 | Living it | What does the new me look like day to day? | The **result**: confident, goal-directed living |

**The whole system in one sentence:** *Picture the real you clearly, give your mind a goal, relax and let it steer, use bad feelings as course-correction signals, and prove the new picture with small daily wins.*

## The 12-week system

Pick **one life area** as your lab (for example: interview confidence) and run 4 phases of 21 days each, matching Maltz's 21-day minimum. Each phase adds themes on top of the last, so by week 12 you use the whole system on one real problem.

| Phase | Weeks | Themes | Daily practice | Proof by day 21 (no re-reading) | Pages to look things up in |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1. See the picture | 1–3 | 1 Self-Image + 4 Beliefs | Write your current self-image in your lab area. Run the four questions (4.3) on one "I can't" belief. Theater of the Mind as your *real* self, **including the obstacle and how you handle it** | Old vs new picture written from memory, one belief dropped, recall test on Themes 1 + 4 | [Self-Image Ladder](self-image-ladder.html), [Self-Image Day](self-image-day.html) |
| 2. Start the engine | 4–6 | 2 Success Mechanism + 3 Imagination | One goal each morning at the edge of your self-image, **as an if-then plan**. Rehearse it with the obstacle. Evening: 3 wins | 21 if-then goals, about 60 wins, recall test on Themes 2 + 3 | [The Machine](psycho-cybernetics-machine.html), [Self-Fulfillment guide](self-fulfillment-reading-guide.html) |
| 3. Calm and repair | 7–9 | 5 Relaxation + 6 Emotions | Quiet room before stressful tasks. Name any FAILURE signal plus its if-then fix. Forgive one old hurt | One scar forgiven, fewer FAILURE signals, recall test on Themes 5 + 6 | [Search for Self-Respect activity](search-for-self-respect-activity.html), [Conquest of Frustration activity](conquest-of-frustration-activity.html) |
| 4. Live it | 10–12 | 7 Living it | Winning feeling before tasks. One courage action per week. Weekly SUCCESS score | A real-world test, a 2-minute teach-back with the book closed, recall on all 7 themes | [Self-Image Code podcast](self-image-code-podcast.html), [Peak](peak.html) |

**Rules that keep it working**

1. Don't add a new book or theme mid-phase. Finish the 21 days first.
2. Missed a day? Correct, don't condemn (2.4). One missed day doesn't stop a habit forming; being very inconsistent does.
3. Judge each phase on day 21, but expect a behaviour to need **about 2 months or more** to feel automatic. Maltz's 21 days is a minimum, not a finish line.
4. **Never re-read to review.** Close the pages and recall, then check.
5. After 12 weeks, keep the daily routine and pick a second lab area.

## What research adds to Maltz

| Finding | What it means here | Source |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Re-reading, highlighting and summarizing are low-value; self-testing and spacing are high-value | Review by **recall**, not re-reading. Re-test at 1 day, 3 days, 1 week, 3 weeks | [Dunlosky et al. 2013](https://www.psychologicalscience.org/news/releases/which-study-strategies-make-the-grade.html); [Roediger & Karpicke 2006](https://psychology.ecu.edu/wp-content/pv-uploads/sites/216/2019/03/Roediger-Karpicke-2006.pdf) |
| Explaining *why* in your own words deepens understanding | Weekly 2-minute teach-back with the book closed | [Fiorella & Mayer 2016](https://link.springer.com/doi/10.1007/s10648-015-9348-9) |
| If-then plans help turn intentions into action | Every goal and every obstacle gets an "If X, then I will Y" plan | [Gollwitzer & Sheeran 2006](https://kops.uni-konstanz.de/entities/publication/2e749bfb-8533-437c-8203-7e788c910c5f); [2024 update](https://www.thebehavioralscientist.com/glossary/implementation-intentions) |
| Positive fantasy alone can lower effort; picturing the wish **plus** the obstacle (WOOP) works better | Theater of the Mind always includes the obstacle and your planned response | [Oettingen / WOOP](https://www.npr.org/transcripts/542426391) |
| Mental practice helps, but the effect is small; it works best alongside real practice | Imagine it, then do a real rep the same week | [Toth et al. 2020](https://pure.ul.ie/en/publications/does-mental-practice-still-enhance-performance-a-24-year-follow-u/) |
| New habits took 66 days on average (range 18–254) | Keep the routine running after day 21 and after week 12 | [Lally et al. 2010](https://bps.org.uk/research-digest/how-form-habit) |

**WOOP in 4 lines (use it for every goal):**

1. **Wish:** what I want in my lab area this week
2. **Outcome:** the best result, pictured vividly (Maltz)
3. **Obstacle:** what *in me* gets in the way (a fear, a habit, a belief)
4. **Plan:** *If [obstacle], then I will [action].*

## Daily 20-minute routine + weekly review

**Morning (5 min)**

- [ ] Write today's one goal as an if-then plan, at the edge of my self-image
- [ ] Read my new-picture sentence once

**Before any stressful moment (2 min)**

- [ ] Quiet room: three slow breaths and a calm place in my mind
- [ ] Recall one past win and its feeling

**Night (13 min)**

- [ ] 3 wins from today, written down
- [ ] Any FAILURE signal? Name it and write its if-then plan
- [ ] 3-question recall: write 3 Maltz ideas from memory, then check
- [ ] 7 min Theater of the Mind: tomorrow's goal done as the real me, **including the obstacle and my if-then response**

**Weekly review (Sunday, 15 min)**

1. Which phase and day am I on?
2. What changed in my lab area this week? One concrete example from real life.
3. Which belief or feeling kept pulling me back? Run the four questions (4.3), then write a new WOOP.
4. Recall test: write every idea I remember from this phase's themes with the pages closed, then check.
5. Teach-back: explain this phase's themes out loud in 2 minutes, book closed.
